About HungerCue

HungerCue is a not-for-profit organization dedicated to fighting food insecurity by connecting local restaurants with homeless shelters — ensuring surplus food feeds people, not landfills.

Our Mission

Combat food waste and food insecurity by creating sustainable connections between restaurants with surplus food and homeless shelters.

Our Vision

A community where no one experiences hunger and surplus food is efficiently redirected to those who need it most.

What We Do

🍽️ Food Redistribution

Collect surplus food from restaurants and deliver it directly to homeless shelters.

🏃 Community Events

Organize 5K walks, holiday drives, and fundraisers to raise awareness and funds.

🤝 Partnership Building

Build lasting partnerships between restaurants, shelters, and community organizations.

📊 Impact Tracking

Measure and report impact — tracking donations, meals provided, and money saved from waste.

Achievements

  • Redirected $4,000+ in surplus food from local restaurants to shelters
  • Organized successful 5K walks and holiday food drives
  • Partnered with 12 restaurants and 4 homeless shelters
  • Provided over 15,000 meals to people in need
  • Received letter of appreciation from Ozanam Shelter Director
